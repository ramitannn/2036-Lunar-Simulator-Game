**2036 Lunar Simulator**
2036 Lunar Simulator is a 2D top-down lunar exploration and survival
game set in the year 2036. Players take on the role of an astronaut
tasked with establishing and maintaining a functional lunar outpost.

The project aims to turn the challenges of preparing the Moon for human
habitation into an accessible, interactive learning experience. Through
gameplay, players are encouraged to think like mission planners, make
meaningful decisions, and consider how real-world conditions can affect
a lunar settlement.

**Project Goals**
The game is designed around four key goals:
- Make trade-offs tangible: Help players understand that mission
  decisions involve balancing limited resources, priorities, and
  risks.
- Use real-world scientific references: Draw on credible lunar and
  space-science resources to inform the game's setting and hazard
  concepts.
- Keep the experience accessible: Present complex ideas through
  approachable, kid-friendly gameplay.
- Encourage replayability: Give players reasons to revisit the
  simulation and explore different approaches to sustaining a lunar
  outpost.
  
**Learning Focus**
The project responds to a common challenge in educational games:
mechanics can become too complex or abstract for younger players to
connect them with real-world applications.

2036 Lunar Simulator aims to make those connections clearer by placing
players in the role of an astronaut responsible for a lunar outpost. The
experience emphasizes practical decision-making and the consequences of
mission planning.

**Scientific References and Data**
The project references NASA resources to support its lunar and
space-weather context.

**NASA DONKI**
The Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information
(DONKI) system, provided by NASA's Community Coordinated Modeling
Center (CCMC), is identified as a scientific reference and potential
data source for simulating realistic space-weather hazards.
- DONKI: https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/
- 
**Lunar QuickMap**
Lunar QuickMap, associated with the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
Camera (LROC), is included as a lunar reference resource.
- Lunar QuickMap: https://quickmap.lroc.im-ldi.com/
Note: These resources are documented as references for the project.
Their mention here does not imply that every resource or dataset is
currently integrated into the playable game.
